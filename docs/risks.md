## 1. Overview
This document identifies and tracks the major risks associated with our project. The purpose of this risk register is to help the team recognize potential problems early, prioritize them based on their impact, and establish plants to address them before they affect project progress. The project involves developing a virtual reality garden environment using Unity and exploring Gaussian Splatting to reconstruct real-world vegetation and garden scenes. Because the project depends on several technologies hardware resources and external decisions, identifying risks early is important for successful development.

## 2. Risk Ranking
Risks are ranked based on their likelihood of occurring and their potential impact on project progress. 
- High: A risk that could significantly delay development, prevent an important feature from functioning, or threaten a project milestone.
- Medium: A risk that could cause noticeable delays or require adjustments to the implementation
- Low: A risk that has a limited impact and can likely be addressed without major changes
